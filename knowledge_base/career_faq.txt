https://www.careers-page.com/rma-group-company-limited

A: Recruitment opportunities may include positions in Administration, Human Resources, Finance, Sales, Marketing, IT, Engineering, Customer Service and other business departments.

## CATEGORY 4: INTERVIEW & RECRUITMENT PROCESS

**Q16. What is the recruitment process at RMA Cambodia?**

A: The recruitment process may include:
1. Application submission
2. CV screening
3. Interview
4. Assessment (if required)
5. Final selection
6. Job offer and onboarding

**Q17. How long does the recruitment process take?**

A: The recruitment timeline depends on the position and hiring requirements. The recruitment team will contact shortlisted candidates regarding the next steps.

**Q18. How will I know if I am shortlisted?**

A: Shortlisted candidates will be contacted by the recruitment team using the contact information provided in their application.

**Q19. What should I prepare for an interview?**

A: Candidates are encouraged to:
- Research the company.
- Review the job description.
- Prepare examples of their skills and experience.
- Dress professionally.
- Arrive on time.

**Q20. Can I reschedule my interview?**

A: If you cannot attend your scheduled interview, please contact the recruitment team as soon as possible to discuss alternative arrangements.
